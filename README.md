The Gilded Hour: a top-down stealth heist game in the browser — plan each night, pack two tools, dodge guard cones and cameras, crack the vault and escape before the alarm peaks, across three escalating nights.

You are Wren, a thief with three jobs in one city: the Meridian Trust Bank, the Pemberton Gallery, and the Crown Reserve Depository. Every night opens on a planning screen — a blueprint of the building, Moth's radio briefing, and six tools to choose two from. Then you are on the floor: guards walk their beats with wall-clipped vision cones and a suspicion meter, cameras sweep, lasers guard the gaps, and the alarm climbs toward a 25-second lockdown. Loot is pocketed through held-E channels and weighs your bag down; the prize waits behind a six-second vault crack; the exit pays for the night.

Guards here do not just patrol and chase: lose one and it comes to the last place it saw you and searches the spot before giving up — so breaking line of sight is only half the escape. Smoke, coins, soft soles and cut wire are how you answer that.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

WASD or arrows move, Shift sneaks, E (hold) works locks and loot, click tosses a coin, Q drops smoke, M mutes. No build step, no network, no assets — all art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesised.
